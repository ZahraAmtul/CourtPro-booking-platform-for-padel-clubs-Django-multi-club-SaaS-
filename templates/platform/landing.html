{# ------------------------------------------------------------------ FAQ #}

Questions owners ask

Do players need to download an app?

No. Players book on your club's website from any phone. They can book as a guest with just a name and mobile number, or create an account to see all their bookings.

Where does online payment money go?

Straight to your own JazzCash merchant account. {{ platform_name }} never holds your money and takes no commission. You can also leave online payment off and take payment at the club.

Who pays for the WhatsApp messages?

Messages are sent from your club's own WhatsApp Business number, so Meta bills your account directly at its standard rates. We help you set up the message templates.

Can I still take walk-ins and phone bookings?

Yes. Click any empty slot on the schedule to add a booking, take the payment, and the invoice is created for you.

What can my reception staff do?

Staff can take bookings, check players in and record payments. Only owners can change prices and settings, export data, cancel invoices or add staff.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes, on the Pro plan. Your booking site can run on an address like book.yourclub.pk instead of {{ "yourclub."|add:domain }}.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Choose a plan and pay by bank transfer. If a payment is late you get seven extra days with a reminder in your dashboard, and your bookings and data are always kept.
